Rules of the game

  1. Roll the dice — its value becomes your current score for the turn.
  2. Keep rolling to add to your current score.
  3. Roll a 1 and you lose your current score, then play passes to the other player.
  4. Press Hold to bank your current score onto your total, then play passes.
  5. First player to reach 100 points wins the game.
